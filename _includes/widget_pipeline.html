Train Delay Pipeline (Digitraffic)

Data pipeline using the Digitraffic API → staged to Bronze/Silver/Gold layers, modeled with dbt, stored in DuckDB, and visualized in Evidence.

Pipeline

Example period: 01.10.2024–03.11.2024; trains 7, 8, 10 (configurable). Data fetched via Digitraffic and staged for processing.

Layers

  • Bronze: Raw with types & surrogate keys.
  • Silver: Detailed stop/train/delay tables.
  • Gold: Final tables for delay analytics & visualization.

Visualization

Weekly average delays (bar) and train-specific trends (line).

Future

Automate selection of period & trains → end-to-end to Evidence.
